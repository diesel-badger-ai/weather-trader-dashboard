    Record coverage

    Skill runs on prediction ⋈ resolution, so what matters while tuning is how fast this fills. A lead bucket with no rows can never reach the sample floor, so its σ cell can never calibrate.

    Of every market the model wanted, which would have filled and what would they have paid? The live book places at most a few orders a cycle, so P&L there is measured on a handful of trades while the model evaluates dozens. This page removes that constraint from the measurement without touching the trading limits.

    Fills are simulated, not assumed: a shadow order counts as filled only where a later book actually crossed its price. A resting maker order is filled disproportionately when the market is moving against it, so assuming every take fills is the most common way a maker backtest invents profit. Expected edge sitting persistently above realised P&L is the signature of that adverse selection rather than of bad luck.
